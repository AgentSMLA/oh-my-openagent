**Bug reports reach the OmO team again.** Every report sent from `/bug`, `/feedback` or the desktop's in-app report was refused by the report service, which the app showed as a connection problem. Reports now carry the exact-body SHA-256 and length and a semantic redactor version, as the service requires, and terminal reports use your `omo login` account when you are signed in; signed-out reports still work. In the terminal, each refusal the service can answer is now named: sign-in refusals point to `omo login`, a request the service rejects shows its error code and request ID, a service-side failure says to try again later, and only a send with no answer is reported as a connection problem ([#11194](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/issues/11194), [#11213](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/11213)).
